**Title:** Chainsaw Man  
**Author:** Tatsuki Fujimoto  
**Genre:** Action, Dark Fantasy, Horror, Comedy  
**Format:** Anime / Manga

## Synopsis
Denji is a poor young man trying to survive by hunting devils with his pet devil **Pochita**.  
After a tragic betrayal, he merges with Pochita and becomes *Chainsaw Man* — a half-human, half-devil who fights for the Public Safety Bureau while chasing his simple dreams of comfort and love.
